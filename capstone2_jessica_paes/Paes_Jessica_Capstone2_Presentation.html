Methodology: Research-Based Approach

How we calculate risk level from personality scores

🤖

What is Machine Learning? Teaching a computer to find patterns in data and make predictions — like how Netflix learns what movies you might like, but here we predict mental health risk from personality traits.

Risk Score Formula

Risk Score = (Neuroticism × 2) − Extraversion − (Conscientiousness × 0.5)

✅ Why This Approach?

  1. Research-backed: Supported by multiple published studies in psychology
  2. Comprehensive: Uses more available data than single-trait approaches
  3. Captures key pattern: The "High Neuroticism + Low Extraversion" combination identified in research
  4. Balanced classes: Tertile classification produces even distribution (~33% each)
  5. Scientifically defensible: Methodology can be justified in academic context
📊

Classification: Respondents are classified into tertiles (thirds) — this is a standard research approach ensuring balanced class distribution.

Machine Learning Models

We tested 5 different prediction methods to find the best one

💡

What is F1-Score? A measure of how accurate the model is (0-100%). Higher = better. It checks both: "Did we find all the high-risk people?" AND "Were our predictions correct?"

Prediction Method Accuracy F1-Score

Gradient Boosting (learns from mistakes) 95.3% 95.3%
