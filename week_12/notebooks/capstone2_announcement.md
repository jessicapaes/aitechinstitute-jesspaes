## 🎯 EXAMPLE PROJECT OUTLINE

**Example: Customer Churn Prediction for Telecom**

**Business Problem:**
"TelcoX loses 20% of customers annually. Build a model to predict which customers will churn next quarter, enabling targeted retention campaigns."

**Data:**
- 7,000 customer records
- 20 features (contract type, monthly charges, tenure, etc.)
- Target: Churned (Yes/No)
- Source: Kaggle Telco Customer Churn

**Models Tried:**
- Logistic Regression (baseline)
- Random Forest
- Gradient Boosting
- Neural Network

**Best Model:**
- Gradient Boosting (XGBoost)
- 85% accuracy, 0.78 F1-score
- Feature importance: Contract type, tenure, monthly charges

**Business Impact:**
"Model identifies high-risk customers with 85% accuracy. Targeting top 20% with retention offers could save $500K annually."
